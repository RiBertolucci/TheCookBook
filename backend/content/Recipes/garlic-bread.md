# Garlic Bread

Crispy garlic-infused bread perfect as a side dish.

## Ingredients
- [Garlic](/api/ingredients/Mediterranei/garlic.md)
## Procedure

1. Mince fresh garlic and mix with softened butter.
2. Slice bread lengthwise and spread garlic butter generously.
3. Sprinkle with fresh parsley and salt.
4. Toast in oven at 350°F for 8-10 minutes until golden.
5. Serve hot.
