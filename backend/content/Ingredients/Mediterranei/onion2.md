# Onion

A layered bulb vegetable that adds depth and sweetness when cooked.

## Properties

- Sharp flavor when raw, sweet when caramelized
- Contains quercetin, an antioxidant
- Good source of fiber and Vitamin C
- Stores well in cool, dry conditions

## Substitutes

- Shallot
- Leek
- Garlic
