# Celery

A crisp, fibrous stalk vegetable commonly used for flavor, texture, and aromatic base in cooking.

## Properties

- Mild, slightly peppery and earthy flavor
- High water content (~95%), very low in calories
- Good source of Vitamin K and folate
- Contains antioxidants such as apigenin
- Crunchy texture when raw, softens when cooked

## Substitutes

- Fennel (for crunch with slight sweetness)
- Bok choy stems
- Green bell pepper (for texture in cooked dishes)
- Celeriac (celery root, for similar flavor)
