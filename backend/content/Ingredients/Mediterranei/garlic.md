# Garlic

A pungent bulb used to flavor countless dishes.

## Properties

- Strong aromatic flavor that mellows when cooked
- Contains allicin, a beneficial sulfur compound
- Essential in many cuisines worldwide
- Available fresh, minced, or powdered

## Substitutes

- Shallot
- Garlic Powder
- Garlic Salt
