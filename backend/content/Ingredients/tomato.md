# Tomato

A vibrant red fruit commonly used in cooking.

## Properties

- Sweet and slightly acidic flavor
- Rich in Vitamin C and potassium
- Versatile ingredient for sauces, salads, and soups
- Best when ripe and at room temperature

## Substitutes

- Red Bell Pepper
- Sun-dried Tomato
- Tomato Paste
